UPSC GS-I Knowledge System

A structured and integrated knowledge resource for General Studies Paper I of the UPSC Civil Services Examination.

The system brings together Indian and world history, the freedom struggle, post-independence developments, Indian and world geography, art and culture, and the major dimensions of Indian society.

How to Use This Knowledge System

  • Study the chapters in sequence to build a connected understanding of the subjects covered in GS-I.
  • Connect historical developments with their geographical, social, cultural and economic contexts.
  • Develop both factual understanding and analytical perspectives while studying each topic.
  • Relate relevant contemporary developments to the static foundations explained in the chapters.
